Core Innovation

Intelligent Anti-Cheat Exam Engine

Ensuring 100% fair and honest testing on mobile phones and computers.

Tab-Switch Detection

Monitors screen visibility using the Page Visibility API. If a student switches to Google or WhatsApp, an immediate alert triggers.

3-Strike Auto Submit

Students receive warnings on suspicious activity. On the 3rd attempt, the exam auto-submits and terminates immediately.

Copy / Paste Lock

Right-click, text selection, inspect element (F12), and keyboard shortcuts (Ctrl+C, Ctrl+V) are strictly blocked.

Speaker Note: "Our examination engine includes a proprietary client-side security layer. Even if students test on mobile phones, switching apps or copying questions is prevented, making the examination 100% credible."
Instant Validation

Instant Results & Certificate Verification

Empowering students with instant feedback and verifiable credentials.

Real-Time Automated Grading

The moment a student submits their exam:

  • Calculates correct answers, wrong answers, and marks.
  • Computes exact percentage and automated grade (A+, A, B, etc.).
  • Stores the complete historical attempt safely in PostgreSQL.

Public Verification Engine

Institutes issue authentic credentials:

  • Auto-generates standardized IDs (e.g., MGI-2026-00001).
  • Employers or schools enter the Roll No or Certificate No.
  • Instantly renders student name, course, score, and issue date.
Speaker Note: "Once the exam concludes, the student receives their grade instantly. Furthermore, any employer can visit our website's 'Verify Certificate' portal to authenticate student diplomas in seconds."
